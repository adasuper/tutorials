Software engineering tutorials

Hands-on tutorials designed for undergraduate software engineering students to learn and practise a range of software development techniques.

Each tutorial walks you through a realistic project step by step, from requirements to working, tested and deployed software. Along the way it explains why each step matters, so you build habits you can carry into your coursework, internships and first job.
